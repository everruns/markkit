Weeknight Lemon Garlic Pasta

Ready in 20 minutes with ingredients you probably already have.

Sponsored: Try MealBox free for 2 weeks
  • Prep: 5 min
  • Cook: 15 min
  • Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 350 g spaghetti
  • 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • 60 ml olive oil
  • 40 g grated parmesan

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti in salted water until al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water.
  2. Warm the olive oil and garlic over low heat until fragrant, about 3 minutes.
  3. Toss the pasta with the garlic oil, lemon zest, lemon juice and parmesan.
  4. Loosen with pasta water as needed and serve immediately.

Notes

For a dairy-free version, replace the parmesan with toasted breadcrumbs.
