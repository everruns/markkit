The Lighthouse of Alexandria was a lighthouse built by the Ptolemaic Kingdom on the island of Pharos.[1] It was one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World.

Contents

  • 1 Construction
  • 2 Destruction

Construction[edit]

Construction was completed during the reign of Ptolemy II Philadelphus. The architect was Sostratus of Cnidus.

Design

The tower had three stages: a square lower section, an octagonal middle section and a circular top.

Destruction[edit]

The lighthouse was badly damaged by earthquakes between 956 and 1323 AD and became an abandoned ruin.
