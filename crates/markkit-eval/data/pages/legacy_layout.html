Pine Hollow Model Railroad Club

Welcome to the home of the Pine Hollow Model Railroad Club! We meet every Tuesday at 7 PM in the basement of the Grange Hall, 18 Mill Road.

Our HO scale layout has over 600 feet of track and models the Boston & Maine railroad in 1952.

New members are always welcome.
Annual dues are $40.
Contact our secretary, Walt Brennan, at walt@pinehollow.test.
